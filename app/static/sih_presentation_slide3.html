Methodology & Implementation Process
  • 1. Raster Ingestion (RasterIngestor): Reads GeoTIFF headers, checks CRS & bounds overlap, calibrates SAR σ⁰ dB and optical reflectance.
  • 2. Intent Classification (QueryClassifier): Extracts linguistic task (VQA, Grounding, Change, Fusion) & keywords from user query.
  • 3. Agentic Routing (AgentOrchestrator): Enforces input-mode constraints and dynamically dispatches to verified specialists in ModelRegistry.
  • 4. Specialist Model Execution: Runs fine-tuned RS-VLM for questions, grounding model for boxes, change detector for difference maps, or optical-SAR fusion.
  • 5. Evidence & Audit (ExecutionTracker): Overlays GeoJSON vector masks on MapLibre map, computes honest confidence, and logs telemetry trace.
