Analysis of Feasibility
  • Technical: Working prototype already functional with native GeoTIFF ingestion, adapted RS-VLM, and React MapLibre GIS.
  • Operational: Zero proprietary API dependencies; runs portably on standard CPU hardware with automatic CUDA GPU acceleration.
  • Economic: Built entirely on open-source libraries (GDAL/PyTorch), eliminating costly per-seat enterprise GIS software licensing fees.
Potential Challenges & Risks
  • Data Heterogeneity: Varying Coordinate Reference Systems (CRS), resolutions (GSD), and band orders across sensors.
  • Atmospheric & Sensor Noise: Optical scenes suffer from cloud cover and shadowing; SAR radar imagery contains speckle noise.
  • AI Hallucination Risk: Standard vision-language models can fabricate confident answers for indistinct or missing satellite features.
Strategies for Overcoming
  • Automated Ingestion: RasterIngestor auto-aligns CRS, resamples pixels, and calibrates SAR σ⁰ dB and optical reflectance.
  • Optical + SAR Synergy: Fuses cloud-penetrating SAR microwave backscatter with optical spectral context, filtering radar speckle.
  • Evidence & Audit: Enforces spatial GeoJSON bounding boxes/masks, honest calibrated confidence, and immutable audit logs.
